The metal read

Anthropic holds 3 of the top 6 and 4 of the top 8 — Opus 4.5 and its variants at 78.0 / 76.5 / 74.3, Sonnet 4.5 at 71.5. The #1→#6 spread is just 3.7 points. The coding frontier isn't a single leader; it's a cluster, and it's mostly one vendor's variance.

That's why metal's Rule 1 pairs the way it does. metal drives with Opus — the top of this exact chart — and the chart earns that choice. But Fable, the navigator, isn't on the chart at all, and that's honest: this index measures coding, and navigation isn't coding. The driver is justified by what you can measure; the navigator is chosen for what this benchmark doesn't test — speed, breadth, and catching what the driver ships.

How to read the confidence marks
